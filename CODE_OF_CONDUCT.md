# Community conduct

This project welcomes careful, curious collaboration. Be respectful, discuss evidence
rather than people, credit prior work, and help newcomers reproduce results.

Do not pressure anyone to flash unverified firmware. Do not share private data,
paid files, credentials, or proprietary binaries. Reports involving security or
personal information should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public
issue.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, and deliberate distribution
of harmful or falsely labeled builds are not accepted. Maintainers may remove
content or participation that puts contributors or devices at risk.
